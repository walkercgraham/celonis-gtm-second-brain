## Capture Guidelines

Each use case note should include:
- **Process area** — what business process
- **Industry** — client sector
- **What was built / found** — the deliverable or insight
- **Timeline & effort** — how long, how many people
- **Outcome / metrics** — quantified impact if known
- **Reusability notes** — what's transferable, what's client-specific
