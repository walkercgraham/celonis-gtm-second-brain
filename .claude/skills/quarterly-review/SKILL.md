# Quarterly Review

Zoom out on your Celonis career. This is about trajectory, not tasks.

## Context

Quarter: $ARGUMENTS (or current quarter if not specified)

## Steps

### Part 1: Gather Quarter Data

1. **Scan all weekly reviews** from the quarter (`01-daily/*-review.md`)
   - Extract patterns that repeated across weeks
   - Note any trajectory changes

2. **Read all client hub files** (`02-areas/celonis/*/*.md`)
   - Where was each client at quarter start vs now?
   - Which engagements closed/won?
   - Which went cold or were lost?

3. **Read career notes** (`02-areas/celonis/_internal/career/`)
   - What was the promotion/career goal?
   - What evidence was generated this quarter?

4. **Read internal meeting notes** (`02-areas/celonis/_internal/meetings/`)
   - Team dynamics and positioning
   - Visibility with leadership

### Part 2: Client Portfolio Analysis

**Client Progression Table:**
| Client | Q Start | Q End | Outcome | Your Contribution |
|--------|---------|-------|---------|-------------------|

For each client:
- Stage progression (discovery → PoV → deal → delivery)
- Revenue/pipeline impact
- What you specifically did that moved it

**Portfolio Health:**
- How many clients are advancing vs stalled?
- Where is pipeline concentrated? (Risk if one deal slips)
- Which clients should you deprioritize?

**Win Rate Analysis:**
- Engagements started vs closed
- Where did you lose and why?
- Patterns in wins vs losses

### Part 3: Skills & Capabilities

**Technical Skills:**
- What new capabilities did you build this quarter?
- What got repeated enough to become a strength?
- What demos/solutions are now in your toolkit?

**Client-Facing Skills:**
- Discovery effectiveness
- Demo delivery
- Stakeholder management
- Executive presence

**Gaps Identified:**
- What's still weak?
- What do peers do that you can't yet?
- What would accelerate your deals if you were better at it?

### Part 4: Visibility & Reputation

**Internal Visibility:**
- Who new knows your work? (Leadership, cross-functional)
- What artifacts exist that others reference?
- Did you contribute to team enablement?

**External Visibility:**
- Customer relationships built
- References or testimonials earned
- Thought leadership or content created

**Reputation Assessment:**
- What are you known for?
- What do you want to be known for?
- Gap between the two?

### Part 5: Promotion Case Progress

If pursuing promotion or next level:

**Evidence Generated:**
| Competency | Evidence This Quarter | Strength |
|------------|----------------------|----------|

- Technical excellence
- Customer impact
- Business acumen
- Leadership/influence

**What's Still Missing:**
- Gaps in the case
- Experiences needed
- Visibility needed

**Timeline Check:**
- On track for target promotion cycle?
- What must happen next quarter to stay on track?

### Part 6: Brutal Honesty

Answer directly:
1. **What did you avoid all quarter?** (There's always something)
2. **Where did you coast instead of push?**
3. **What would your manager say is your gap?**
4. **If you continue exactly as-is, where is your career in 1 year?**

### Part 7: Next Quarter Priorities

**One career goal:** What's the single most important professional outcome?

**Client focus:** Which 2-3 clients deserve disproportionate attention?

**Skill to develop:** What capability would have the highest ROI?

**Visibility action:** One thing to increase your profile internally or externally

## Output

Create a quarterly review note at `02-areas/celonis/_internal/reviews/YYYY-QX-quarterly.md` with all sections.

Output a summary to screen focusing on:
- Biggest wins (with evidence)
- Biggest gaps (with honesty)
- Clear priorities for next quarter

**Tone example:**

> "Q1 was a 7/10. You closed [Client A] AR and got [Client B] to confirm the [value] premium — those are real wins with customer evidence. But [Client C] has been 'almost ready' for 6 weeks without shipping.
>
> The promotion case is thin on 'technical leadership' — you're executing well but not visibly leading. Next quarter needs a demo or artifact that others reference.
>
> You're spread across 8 clients but only 3 are actually moving. Ruthless prioritization needed — pick 3, let the others go to maintenance mode.
>
> Q2 focus: Ship [Client C], generate promotion evidence, go deep on [Client B] (highest TCV potential)."
